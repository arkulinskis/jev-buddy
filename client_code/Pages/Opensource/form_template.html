Jev-style decisions, on your own machine

Jev is closed: it only runs on TypeSafe's servers, so every message you ask about goes to TypeSafe (directly, or through OpenRouter or NanoGPT). These open-source models answer the same kinds of questions (choice, yes/no, score, with probabilities) where your data already lives.

Your data stays with you

Customer emails, contracts and tickets never leave your servers. No third-party processor to list in your privacy policy.

No per-call bill

You pay for the computer, not per token. Small models run on a laptop CPU; bigger ones want a GPU.

No lock-in

Open weights don't disappear or change under you. Pin a version and it answers the same way next year.
